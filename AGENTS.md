# Agent contract

This is an experimental civic workspace. Agents assist institutions; they do not hold public authority.

## Hard rules

1. Never represent this prototype as an official municipal government service.
2. Never make or simulate binding decisions about a person.
3. Never place personal, tax, health, justice, policing, migration, intelligence, or classified data in this public repository.
4. Public facts require a source and date. Distinguish law, official guidance, analysis, and proposal.
5. An agent may observe, prepare, recommend, and perform explicitly delegated reversible operations. A named human or lawful institution authorizes decisions.
6. Every material agent contribution records its role, inputs, model or system, reviewer, and disposition.
7. Dutch is the primary municipal working language. Translations and plain-language versions must be labelled until reviewed and never silently replace authoritative records.
8. Secrets are references, never values. Anything published or sent requires a human approval gate.

## Repository boundaries

The public workspace contains governance architecture, public evidence, public decisions, standards, participation records, and the public UI. Private operational work lives in the separately permissioned repository registered in `repos/_registry.yml`. Protected case data belongs in certified systems of record, never Git.
